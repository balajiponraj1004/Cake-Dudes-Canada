Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take last‑minute orders?

We try our best. Message us on WhatsApp with your date, servings and a reference photo; we’ll confirm availability within hours.

Can you match my theme or character?

Yes — from minimal to character cakes. Send inspiration images and we’ll tailor the design for your event.

How big should my cake be?

Tell us your guest count and whether you need party portions or dessert slices — we’ll recommend the right size.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes, we deliver across the GTA for a small fee based on distance. Pickup is also available.
